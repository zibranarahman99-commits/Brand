Rahman Home is a small online storefront for practical home and kitchen essentials. We focus on useful products, clear communication and straightforward ordering. Every order is confirmed with you before it is sent to fulfilment.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

How does delivery work?

Delivery times and charges depend on the supplier and your PIN code. We confirm both before accepting your order.

Can I pay online?

Yes, the site supports a UPI payment hand-off after you configure your UPI ID in config.js. Payment is not automatically verified by this static website; confirm receipt in your own UPI/bank app before asking a supplier to dispatch.

What if an item arrives damaged?

Contact us promptly with your order details and photos. The applicable return or replacement terms will be confirmed before purchase.

Are all items always in stock?

Availability can change. We check stock with the supplier before confirming your order.
